Offline UPI Payment Protocol

Mesh-Networked Transactions

An offline, decentralized payment gateway enabling cryptographically signed transactions through device-to-device mesh networking without active internet access.

The Problem

Traditional online payment flows depend on an active network connection. The project explores how a payment packet can travel between nearby devices through a mesh and reach a backend later, while protecting the packet and preventing duplicate settlement.

The Solution

The system combines signed payment packets, hybrid encryption, device-to-device forwarding, a bridge ingestion point, and idempotent settlement. The backend verifies and processes the transaction when the bridge reconnects to the server.

Sender→Encrypted Payment Packet→Mesh Network→Bridge→Spring Boot Backend→Settlement→Ledger

Key Engineering Features

Hybrid Encryption

RSA-OAEP is used for asymmetric key exchange while AES-GCM protects packet-level data.

Mesh Forwarding

Payment packets can move between devices before reaching a bridge.

Bridge Ingestion

The bridge forwards collected packets to the backend for processing.

Idempotent Settlement

Duplicate-payment protection helps prevent repeated settlement during synchronization.

Architecture

At a high level, a sender creates a payment packet, the packet is protected cryptographically, nearby devices forward it through the mesh, and a bridge eventually sends it to the Spring Boot backend. The backend validates the packet and updates the ledger.
